How it works

Four steps, and you only do the first and third.

The hard parts — US charitable rules, Indian regulation, currency, verification — happen behind the account.

01

Open and contribute

Move cash or appreciated stock into your account, held by an established US public charity. You get the proper acknowledgment for your records.

02

The balance keeps working

While you decide, the charitable balance sits in approved investment options rather than idle. Growth stays dedicated to giving, and value can go down as well as up.

03

Recommend a grant

Choose from Indian programs we've checked for registration, governance, banking, and their ability to actually report back. You recommend; the charity approves and sends.

04

See what it did

Funds go straight to the organization's designated account in India. Then you get the transfer record, the delivery evidence, and the outcome — dated and sourced.

Proof, not photographs

Every claim comes with its receipts.

A picture of a smiling child is marketing. We grade what we show you, and we tell you what each level does and doesn't prove.

Straight answers

What we will and won't say.

Giving products earn trust by being precise about the unglamorous parts. So, plainly:

What Diya does

  • Shows every fee, exchange rate, and expected timeline in dollars before you commit
  • Sends funds directly to each organization's designated account in India, with no middle stop
  • Re-checks registration, banking, and sanctions immediately before any money moves
  • Keeps your name, balance, and grant amounts private unless you choose otherwise
  • Protects the dignity of the people served — no identifiable faces without documented consent

What Diya won't claim

  • That this is a tax-free personal account, or that any deduction is guaranteed
  • That you own or control the balance once it's contributed — legally, the charity does
  • That 100% of your money reaches the field, when banking and verification cost real money
  • That your specific dollar bought one specific meal, when funds are pooled
  • That we'll ever sell, rent, or broker your data. Not now, not at scale
