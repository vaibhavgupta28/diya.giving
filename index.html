How it works

Four steps. You do the first and the third.

US charitable rules, Indian regulation, currency, and verification all happen behind the account.

01

Contribute

Move cash or shares into your account, held by an established US public charity. The tax side is handled once, at the start, and you get the paperwork for your records.

02

It keeps working

While you decide, the balance sits in approved investment options rather than idle. Growth stays dedicated to giving. Value can go down as well as up.

03

Choose where it goes

Pick from Indian organisations we've checked for registration, banking, governance, and whether they can actually report back. You recommend; the charity approves and sends.

04

See what it did

Transfer record, delivery evidence, and outcome, dated and sourced. Then the next release depends on it.

A note on faith-linked giving, because families ask. We can support the charitable work a temple or trust runs, such as a school, a clinic, or a kitchen, where that work sits in a registered entity cleared to receive funds from abroad. We can't route money to worship itself, and we can't arrange a named puja or a plaque, because a gift that buys you something personal isn't a charitable grant.

Straight answers

What we will and won't say.

Giving products earn trust by being precise about the unglamorous parts. So, plainly:

What Diya does

  • Shows every fee, exchange rate, and expected timeline in dollars before you commit
  • Sends funds directly to each organisation's designated account in India, with no middle stop
  • Re-checks registration, banking, and sanctions immediately before any money moves
  • Holds the next release until the last one has been reported and reviewed
  • Keeps your name, balance, and amounts private unless you choose otherwise
  • Protects the dignity of the people served. No identifiable faces without documented consent

What Diya won't claim

  • That this is a tax-free personal account, or that any deduction is guaranteed
  • That you own or control the balance once it's contributed. Legally, the charity does
  • That 100% reaches the field, when banking and verification cost real money
  • That your specific dollar bought one specific meal, when funds are pooled
  • That you can sponsor one named child. We fund the programme that keeps a class in school
  • That we'll ever sell, rent, or broker your data. Not now, not at scale
