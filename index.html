Fair questions

Why not just do what you're doing now?

Most people we've spoken to are already giving to India somehow. Here's the honest comparison, including where the other option is fine.

Why not send money to family to hand over?

Because you can't deduct it, and you'll never see a report. It's fast and it's personal, and for a relative in trouble it's the right call. But it isn't charitable giving in the eyes of the IRS, and there is no record of what the money did.

Why not use an Indian giving website?

They're built for rupees, not for your US tax return. They do a good job inside India. What they can't give an American taxpayer is a deduction, a way to give shares instead of cash, or a balance that grows while you decide.

Why not use the big US giving account I already have?

You can, and we'll take grants from it. What they don't have is India. They can't tell you whether an organisation's clearance is current this week, or chase a report from a district office in Odisha. That's the whole of what we do.

Why not give to a US charity that works in India?

Often you should, and several are on our list. The limit is choice. You get their portfolio, not yours, and you rarely find out what your specific gift funded. We'll happily route you to one when it's the better answer.

What makes you different, in one line?

We're the only one that withholds the next release until the last one is accounted for. Everyone can move money. Almost nobody makes the money conditional on evidence, because it's slow, unpopular with organisations, and expensive to run.

Straight answers

What we will and won't say.

Giving products earn trust by being precise about the unglamorous parts. So, plainly:

What Diya does

  • Shows every fee, exchange rate, and expected timeline in dollars before you commit
  • Sends funds directly to each organisation's designated account in India, with no middle stop
  • Re-checks registration, banking, and sanctions immediately before any money moves
  • Holds the next release until the last one has been reported and reviewed
  • Tells you when the cheaper or simpler option is somebody else's product
  • Protects the dignity of the people served. No identifiable faces without documented consent

What Diya won't claim

  • That this is a tax-free personal account, or that any deduction is guaranteed
  • That you own or control the balance once it's contributed. Legally, the charity does
  • That 100% reaches the field, when banking and verification cost real money
  • That your specific dollar bought one specific meal, when funds are pooled
  • That you can sponsor one named child. We fund the programme that keeps a class in school
  • That you stay anonymous to India. Indian rules now require donors behind cross-border gifts to be named in an organisation's annual return, and we'll tell you before you give
